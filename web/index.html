🔍 Roundtrip Decoder & Balanced-Ternary Gate (CT-000)

Every <~~0o0 frame carries a 9-trit balanced-ternary ({-1:T, 0:0, +1:1}) checksum and net charge, plus CRC32. These detect accidental corruption; they do not authenticate a sender, verify a research claim or demonstrate radio delivery.
